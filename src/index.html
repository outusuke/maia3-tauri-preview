♞ Maia3 Chess

Play a local Maia-3 engine and, optionally, grade your games with Stockfish. Maia-3 runs on ONNX Runtime — a light, fast-starting engine with no PyTorch to install or keep around.

Maia-3 models

Install any size you want to play against — each is independent, and the Play tab lets you pick between whichever ones you have.

Stockfish
